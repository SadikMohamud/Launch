Architecture and interiors

Considered spaces for considered people

We design buildings that age well, using materials chosen for how they weather rather than how they photograph on the day they are finished.

Recent work

A short selection of projects completed across the last four years, ranging from a coastal house to a converted grain store.

Grain Store

A nineteenth century store rebuilt as a single family home, keeping the original roof structure exposed throughout.

Coastal House

A low house set into a dune, arranged so every room opens onto sheltered ground rather than onto the wind.

Harbour Offices

Workspace for a fishing cooperative, built in blockwork and larch to survive constant salt exposure.

Working together

We take on a small number of projects each year so that each one receives the attention it needs from first sketch to final handover.
